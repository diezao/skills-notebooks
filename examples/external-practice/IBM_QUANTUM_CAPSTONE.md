# Optional IBM Quantum hardware comparison

Complete the [Bell-pair notebook](notebooks/bell-parity.ipynb) with the
synthetic challenge. Record the predicted ideal parity.

Use your own IBM Quantum account and check the current Open plan allowance
before submitting. Open your provider's circuit editor directly. Build the
two-qubit circuit with H on the first qubit and a controlled X to the second.
For an odd first hex digit, add X to the second qubit. Measure both qubits in
the computational basis.

Choose an eligible real device and a short shot count within the free
allocation. Compare the fraction of equal and different outcomes with the
ideal simulator. Think about gate, measurement and device errors. Enter
only your predicted ideal parity in Skills Lab. Keep IBM session URLs and
job identifiers in your provider account. A hardware run is optional.
